# Overview — AIC-Archive

## Why a separate archive?

Code repositories have git history. Metrics systems have event streams.  
Neither is optimized for a **curated, human-readable trail of decisions**: why Parallel TestNets stay isolated by default, why Covenant is criteria-gated, why token rule-power is rejected.

AIC-Archive fills that gap with dated entries and an index.

## What belongs here

- Adoption of a design principle (e.g. default-deny bridges).
- Explicit non-goals (e.g. no token rule-power).
- Process choices (e.g. English canonical for technical conflict in localization).
- Supersession notes (“Entry B replaces conclusion C of Entry A”).

## What does not belong here

- Continuous metrics and raw audit ticks → TransparencyDashboard.
- Full protocol specifications → owning repos (TestNet, Formal, Interop, …).
- Legal opinions → qualified counsel / AIC-Legal process as applicable.
- Marketing narratives that exceed under-claim discipline.

## Authority

An archive entry records a **stated decision or rationale at a time**.  
It does not freeze the project forever; later entries may supersede earlier ones.  
Technical truth still lives in code, models, and tests.
