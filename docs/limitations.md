# Limitations

1. **Not cryptographically immutable** — this is a git-hosted log with social append discipline, not a blockchain.
2. **Not complete** — only curated decisions; absence of an entry does not mean nothing happened.
3. **Not legal authority** — entries do not create immunity, compliance, or regulatory determinations.
4. **Not a substitute for code** — implementations may lag or diverge; verify in the owning repository.
5. **Pre-Covenant** — nothing here declares mainnet or Covenant.
6. **Entity ≠ immunity** — archiving a decision does not shield any person or organization from law or criticism.
7. **Under-claim** — read non-claims sections; do not quote entries as stronger than they are.
