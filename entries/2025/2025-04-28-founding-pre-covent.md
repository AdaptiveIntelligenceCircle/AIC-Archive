# Founding posture: pre-Covenant

**Pre-Covenant · under-claim · not legal advice**

## Metadata

- Date: 2025-04-28 (founding-period orientation for the archive)
- Related: AIC-Covenant criteria materials, public org posture

## Context

Multiple repositories and experiments can be mistaken for a declared main network or finished product.

## Decision / statement

AIC’s public technical posture remains **pre-Covenant** until explicit declaration criteria are met.  
Existence of repositories, TestNets, formal models, or documentation **does not** constitute Covenant declaration or mainnet launch.

## Rationale

- Prevents social pressure from “shipping” a narrative ahead of checklist reality.
- Keeps Parallel TestNet and research artifacts in the experimental category.
- Aligns external communication with internal go-live discipline.

## Non-claims

This entry does **not**:

- list the full Covenant checklist (see AIC-Covenant)
- schedule a declaration date
- invalidate experimentation or publication of code
