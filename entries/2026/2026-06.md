# Localization: English canonical on technical conflict

**Pre-Covenant · under-claim · not legal advice**

## Metadata

- Date: 2026-10-06
- Related repositories: AIC-Localization

## Context

Orientation materials are being translated (starting with Vietnamese) so more readers can access AIC boundaries.  
Translations can drift in meaning if treated as independent authorities.

## Decision / statement

**English is the canonical language** for technical precision and normative conflict resolution.  
Translations are aids to understanding.  
When a translation and the English source diverge on technical or policy meaning, the English source prevails until the translation is corrected.  
“Not legal advice” and pre-Covenant notices must be preserved in every language.

## Rationale

- Single conflict-resolution rule reduces ambiguity for implementers.
- Protects under-claim and fail-closed wording from accidental strengthening in translation.
- Still allows Global South accessibility via reviewed locale packs.

## Non-claims

This entry does **not**:

- make English a legal requirement in any jurisdiction
- deprecate translations
- claim that any translation is complete or official for regulators
