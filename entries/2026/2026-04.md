# Orientation: Third Path, under-claim, no token rule-power

**Pre-Covenant · under-claim · not legal advice**

## Metadata

- Date: 2026-04-01 (orientation anchor for archive; approximate public framing period)
- Related: MyVision, Whitepaper, Start-Here

## Context

AIC needed a stable public description of what it refuses and what it prioritizes, without sounding like a finished product or a political franchise.

## Decision / statement

Public orientation includes:

1. **Third Path** — refuse pure state capture *and* pure commercial rule-power over the protocol.
2. **Under-claim** — prefer accurate, limited statements over maximum hype.
3. **No token rule-power** — tokens or payment weight do not buy protocol law.
4. **Entity ≠ immunity** — participation creates no legal shield.
5. **Fail-closed / human gates** — design intent near authorization paths.

## Rationale

Without explicit non-goals, open projects drift toward token governance theater or over-claim marketing.  
Stating refusals early makes later technical choices (Covenant criteria, bridge default-deny, localization rules) coherent.

## Non-claims

This entry does **not**:

- complete a political theory
- guarantee capture-resistance in deployment
- declare any component production-ready
- provide legal advice
